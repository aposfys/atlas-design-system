VANE.

A dense, rounded interface language built on glass, in two themes. Panels are translucent and blurred; the light catches their top edge instead of casting a shadow. One chartreuse carries every action.

01 Palette

These are the semantic roles, not the raw values — every swatch is a var() that repoints when the theme flips. Components may only use this layer. Each chip sits on a light/dark split substrate, so translucent tokens read as translucent instead of vanishing into the panel. The accent is deliberately two tokens: --fill-accent for backgrounds and --text-accent for type, because chartreuse as type on a pale ground measures 1.3:1.

08 Iconography

Phosphor, regular weight only — never mixed with bold, fill or duotone. Icons inherit currentColor and are sized in em so they track the label beside them. They never appear alone without an accessible name, and they never carry meaning on their own: a state is a tag plus a word, not a glyph.
