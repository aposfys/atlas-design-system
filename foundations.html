Twelve columns, gutter --sp-4 (14px — the grid's own unit, ×8). Reading surfaces cap at 1060px; application shells run to 1440px and then stop — a ledger wider than 1440 stops being scannable. The canonical splits are 8/4 and 3×4; a 6/6 split is a smell, usually two views pretending to be one.

One switch, applied to a region, never to a single control. Dense drops row padding one step and controls to 28px. Type sizes never change — density is spacing, not shrinking. Dense is for pointer surfaces; the 44px touch floor makes it unavailable on touch.

04 ELEVATION
0 GROUND-DEEP
1 GLASS
2 GLASS-LG · FRAME
3 SOLID · OVERLAY

Four levels, and light does the work of shadow: each level catches more of the top-edge sheen, none casts anything downward. Level 3 is opaque because dialogs and menus must be readable over anything. There is no level 4 — anything above a dialog is a design error.

05 MOTION
--d-1 · 90msmicroHover, press, focus, toggle — feedback on the control itself. Below perception as animation; reads as response.
--d-2 · 170msstructuralPanels, dialogs, tab content, rows entering — anything that changes the layout.
--easeone curvecubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) — fast out, long settle. One curve for everything; variety of easing is how motion systems rot.
reduced1msprefers-reduced-motion collapses both durations. Nothing in the system depends on seeing the transition.
06 DATA COLOUR
series 1 — the accent leads series 2 series 3 series 4 series 5 series 6

Six series tokens, re-derived per theme — every one clears 3:1 against its ground in both themes. The order is fixed: series 1 is always the accent, and a chart never reorders the palette to look nicer. Past six series, aggregate. Colour is never the only carrier — a legend names every series, and lines differ in weight as well as hue.
