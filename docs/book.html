ATLAS is a product interface system, not a brand — there is no photography and no logotype beyond the mark. Its character lives in three structural devices: the index label, the glass panel, and the rule.

Two laws sit above everything. Components are built from the semantic token layer only — any raw hex in the component layer is a bug. And every measured claim is enforced: tools/check_contrast.py re-measures all 34 declared colour pairs in both themes on every run.

01 PHILOSOPHY

The three devices

01 THE INDEX LABEL

A 10px tracked mono label with a numbered accent prefix and a hairline running to the end of the column. Every block of content opens with one.

02 GLASS

Panels are translucent white over the navy with an 18px blur. A 1px sheen along the top edge stands in for elevation; nothing casts a shadow downward.

03 THE RULE

Inside glass, content separates with hairlines, never with nested cards. Figures sit in one divided panel, not in separate tiles. Tables are the primary surface, with an accent-tint hover instead of striping.

Three things were inherited from the reference system and everything visual is new: the 12–14px density, the 1.75px base grid, and mono numerics for every figure, reference and identifier.

The jump is the character

A SPLINE SANS MONO · THE LABEL

Everything numeric — figures, references, identifiers — is Spline Sans Mono, tabular. The system's character is the jump between 10px tracked labels and 46px tabular figures, with very little in between.

B GABARITO · THE INTERFACE

Gabarito carries headings, body and controls at 12–14px — dense on purpose; nothing is inflated to a comfortable-looking 16px. Scale: 10 / 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 16 / 19 / 25 / 33 / 46.

Weights 400–700; tight tracking (−0.03em) on display sizes, 0.1em tracking on the 10px labels.

05 ICONOGRAPHY

Phosphor, regular, and six rules

  • 1Regular weight onlyNever mixed with bold, fill or duotone — the mix is the fastest way to make a set look borrowed.
  • 2Sized in em1.15em in line; standalone at 14 / 16 / 20 / 24px.
  • 3currentColorAn icon takes the colour of the text it sits with, never its own.
  • 4Never aloneIcon-only controls carry aria-label; decorative glyphs carry aria-hidden.
  • 5Never the sole carrierA state is a tint plus a word; the icon repeats what the copy says.
  • 6No emoji

07 GLASS

Depth without a single shadow

01 A PANEL OVER LIGHT

The blur needs light behind it — dropped onto a flat fill, glass reads as grey mush.

UPTIME99.96%
P5084ms
  • The sheen is the elevationA 1px gradient along the top edge stands in for a shadow. The one box-shadow in the system is the glow on a hovered primary button.
  • The budget is a numberSix simultaneously visible blurred panels per view, the app frame counted once. Beyond that, group under one panel with hairlines.
  • The one forbidden thingGlass never nests inside glass. Controls that live inside a glass panel — the theme toggle, the segmented control — are solid.
  • When blur is gone@supports raises the fill opacity; forced-colors mode drops the blooms, sets glass to solid Canvas and hands the palette to the system.

10 IN USE

Three products, one language

Deliberately different products — a sensor fleet, a finance close, a public status page — every one composed from components.css alone. To reskin for a new product: change the accent trio and the ground pairs, re-derive the text accent, run the gate. The semantic layer and every component follow untouched.
